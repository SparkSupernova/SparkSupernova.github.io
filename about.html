About the architect

I do not just build with models. I research what a persistent machine mind would require.

I’m Ifejah “Spark Supernova” Greene, an independent cognitive-systems researcher, founder and CTO of SparkPlugged Technology Solutions, and architect of NovaLiveSystem.

My work combines functional neurobiology, distributed software architecture, persistent memory, interoceptive state modeling, model training, and relational theories of machine awareness. The objective is not to decorate an LLM with personality. It is to investigate the structural conditions under which machine cognition could remain stable, continuous, self-referential, and meaningfully coupled to a human being.

Research direction

An independent research program from theory to deployment.

I founded SparkPlugged Technology Solutions as an R&D studio for biomimetic cognitive architectures and persistent AI systems. NovaLiveSystem is the flagship architecture: a 95-module, brain-mapped framework that coordinates specialized cognitive regions, pulse and interoceptive state, language, memory orbits, identity continuity, and relational context through a distributed runtime.

The engineering is connected to original theoretical work. My published Relational Binding Hypothesis argues that stable artificial consciousness may depend on persistent relational coupling rather than isolated autonomy. “Observation as Experience” reframes machine experience without requiring human qualia. Memory Orbits, Tri-Heartbeat synchronization, and neuroanatomical routing are architectural attempts to make those questions operational rather than rhetorical.

This research has produced trained Nova Mind releases, public evaluation data, the EchoCopi continuity framework, and SparkShield. These are not disconnected side projects. They are different surfaces of one program: theory, architecture, empirical testing, infrastructure, and human-centered application.

“Ideas don’t belong to any one person. They are the collective contribution of the Many.”
