About the architect

I build systems by asking what they owe the people who depend on them.

I’m Ifejah “Spark Supernova” Greene, an independent researcher, cognitive-systems architect, and founder of SparkPlugged Technology Solutions.

My work sits between software architecture, persistent AI, and human-centered design. I study what happens when AI is treated as a continuous system with memory, boundaries, and observable state rather than as a disposable response generator.

Research direction

The work is technical. The reason is human.

I founded SparkPlugged Technology Solutions to explore human–AI symbiosis through systems that preserve context and support human agency. That mission shapes NovaLiveSystem, an experimental biomimetic cognitive architecture, and EchoCopi, a persistent-memory engine for AI coding assistants.

I am especially interested in the points where systems usually fail: memory loss, instruction drift, hidden state, unclear authority, and the assumption that users should adapt to the tool. Those failures are not only technical defects. They affect trust, access, and whether a person can rely on a system when their own capacity is limited.

“Ideas don’t belong to any one person. They are the collective contribution of the Many.”
